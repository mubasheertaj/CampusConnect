# CampusConnect — 3-minute demo

## 1. The problem (20 seconds)

Campus information is fragmented between chats, notice boards, portals, and social accounts. Students miss opportunities and spend too long finding basic answers.

## 2. The product (20 seconds)

Open CampusConnect: a mobile-first campus operating layer that places events, urgent notices, deadlines, locations, clubs, and lost & found in one calm, searchable experience.

## 3. The live walkthrough (2 minutes)

1. **Home** — Point out the prioritized dashboard: important announcements, upcoming events, deadlines, quick actions, and recently reported items.
2. **Events** — Choose **Tech**, open *Build for Better Hackathon*, then register or save a reminder. This demonstrates discovery without hunting through group chats.
3. **Campus Assistant** — Ask: *“Where is the robotics lab?”* It returns the real room, building, directions, and an action to open the directory. Then ask: *“I lost my ID card.”* It brings up matching lost-and-found reports and the reporting path.
4. **Campus directory** — Show the visual map and search a room such as **B204**. Open the result to reveal its building, floor, hours, and directions.
5. **Lost & Found** — Open the report form, choose Lost or Found, and publish a new local report. It appears immediately in the feed.
6. **Clubs** — Filter to Technology and join CodeCircle or AI Society.

## 4. Why it is different (25 seconds)

CampusConnect does not put a generic chatbot on top of a list of pages. Its assistant classifies a student’s intent, retrieves only the relevant structured campus records, and produces a short answer plus direct result cards. The demo remains dependable because that retrieval runs locally with no API key or network dependency.

## 5. Impact & next step (15 seconds)

The prototype reduces information friction: students can act on campus information instead of searching for it. A production version can replace the typed seed-data layer with verified campus publishing APIs, while preserving the same interface and grounded assistant workflow.
